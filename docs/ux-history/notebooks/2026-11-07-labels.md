# Notebooks — labels instead of notebooks (2026-11-07, Android)

Tag: `ux/notebooks/2026-11-07`

Notebooks are gone: a moment has any number of labels (none included). The language, the known voices, the guess by
time and the folder on disk, which notebooks held, moved: each moment keeps its language (picked on the Record bar),
voices are one list for the app, the guess suggests labels, and every moment has its own folder whatever its labels.

## Options
- **a — Chips on top, labels on the Record bar**: chips filter by a label or No label (n); each moment shows all its
  labels. The Record bar has the next moment's labels (the guessed ones, ✦, already on; + or a tap changes them) and a
  language chip. Moments without labels wait on top with "Add <label>" for the one their time suggests and Labels….
  Holding moments selects them; the tag icon opens a sheet with a checkbox per label (a dash when only some have it),
  New label, and a pencil to rename or delete one. *Picked*, promoted in PROMOTED.
- **b — Labels after, one list**: Record has only the language; every moment in one list by time, an unlabeled one
  with "✦ Add <label>", a tag icon on every row, and a filter button in the top bar for one or more labels. *Turned
  down*.
- **c — A side drawer**: ☰ lists All moments, No labels and every label with its count and a pencil, like mail
  folders; the Record bar as in a. *Turned down*.

## What they faked
- Labels and moments' labels (built from the lab notebooks, plus an "Exam" label on two moments).
- The label guess (Psychology on the Monday before class; none on Tuesday evening, `a-no-guess`) and the suggestion
  for a moment without labels.
- Recording (a clock), the speaker pass after it; labels, renames and deletes in memory.
- Not shown: labels on the moment's own screens.
