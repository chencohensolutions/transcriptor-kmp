## Options
- **a — A menu on each lecture**: ⋮ at the end of every row with Delete…, which asks first (its recording,
  transcript and notes go from the lectures folder, for good). *Turned down*.
- **b — Hold to select, Undo**: holding a lecture selects it and the top bar becomes "2 selected" with ✕ and a trash
  can; a tap adds or removes lectures while selecting. Delete takes them off the list at once with "2 lectures deleted ·
  Undo"; the files go when the snackbar closes. *Picked*.
- **c — Swipe to delete, Undo**: slide a row either way over red and a trash can; Undo as in b. *Turned down*.

## What they faked
- The lab notebooks on the Monday before class (as `notebooks current`); deleting only took lectures out of memory.
- Not shown: deleting a lecture while a pass runs on it.
